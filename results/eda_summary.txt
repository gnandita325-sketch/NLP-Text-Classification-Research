7. Text Preprocessing Observations

- Messages were converted to lowercase.
- Extra whitespace was normalized.
- Punctuation was retained because it may provide useful classification signals.
- 138 messages contained URL-related patterns.
- 1,464 messages contained numerical characters.
- 94.78% of spam messages contained numbers.
- 15.66% of ham messages contained numbers.
- Numbers and URLs were retained for further model evaluation.

8. Word Frequency Analysis

Ham messages:
- Frequently occurring words include i, you, to, the, u, my, and me.
- Conversational words appear frequently.

Spam messages:
- Frequently occurring words include to, call, your, free, txt, ur, and mobile.
- Promotional and communication-related words appear frequently.

Observation:
- Ham messages show more conversational language.
- Spam messages contain several promotional and marketing-related terms.
- Word frequency may provide useful features for text classification.
